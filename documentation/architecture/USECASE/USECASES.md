# USECASE

Monitor the audio channel of remote interviewer(s) who will be using a meeting applications such as Microsoft Teams or similar. The audio will be fed to AI which will respond with appropriate assistance to answer the interviewers questions. An important aspect is that the remote interviewer(s) should not necessarily be aware that the audio channel is being monitored in this way.



The application should monitor the microphone and transcribe to the screen. In another window it should out put detected questions or imperative sentences.
